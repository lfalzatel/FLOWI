Log expenses by talking and know how much you can spend today.

• Log by voice: say one or several expenses at once, no typing.
• Receipt photo: snap a picture and FLOWI fills in the details.
• Import your statement: bring in your bank's transactions.

• What you can spend today: FLOWI tells you how much is left for the day based on your income and expenses.

• Debts and payments: keep track of what you owe and what others owe you.

• Emergency fund and savings goals, with your progress in percent.
